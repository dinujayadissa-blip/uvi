Privacy, Terms & Cookies

Last updated: 15 September 2026

Please note: Wander Australia is a demonstration website. It does not operate as a real business, collect payments, or send communications. The policies below describe how a live version would handle your information and are provided as a template only.

Privacy Policy

If this were a live service, we would collect only the information you choose to give us — such as your name, email address, and trip preferences submitted through our contact form. We would use it solely to send you the travel ideas you requested.

We would never sell your personal data. You would be able to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time by contacting us. We would retain your details only as long as needed to provide the service, then delete them.

A live service handling visitors from the EU/UK would comply with the GDPR, and visitors from California with the CCPA, including honouring data-subject rights and lawful bases for processing.

Content such as destinations, itineraries, and travel tips is general guidance and may not reflect current conditions, prices, or entry requirements. Always confirm details with official sources (for example, government visa and travel-advisory websites) before making travel plans.

We make no warranty as to the accuracy or completeness of the content and accept no liability for decisions made based on it.

Cookie Notice

This demo site does not set tracking or advertising cookies. A live version would tell you clearly which cookies it uses (for example, analytics), ask for your consent where required, and let you manage your preferences.

Contact

Questions about these policies? In a live service you would reach us at a published contact address. As this is a demo, no contact channel is monitored.
