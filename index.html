Before you go

Travel essentials

The practical stuff — sorted, so you can focus on the fun part.

🛂

Visa & entry

Most visitors need an eVisitor or ETA visa, arranged online before departure. Apply at least a few weeks ahead.

☀️

Best time to visit

Seasons are flipped from the Northern Hemisphere. Sep–Nov and Mar–May offer mild weather nationwide.

💵

Currency

Australian Dollar (AUD). Cards are accepted almost everywhere — carry only a little cash for remote areas.

🚙

Getting around

Distances are huge — domestic flights save time. Renting a car is best for road trips and remote regions.

🩹

Health & safety

Tap water is safe to drink. Sun protection is essential — the UV index runs high nearly year-round.

🔌

Plugs & power

Type I plug, 230V. Bring an adapter — most other regions' plugs won't fit.

Insider tips

Travel like a local

01

Book Uluru, the Whitsundays and Tasmania's Overland Track well ahead in peak season (Jun–Aug for the north, Dec–Feb for the south).

02

Tipping isn't expected — service staff are paid a fair wage. Round up or tip ~10% only for exceptional service.

03

Always swim between the red and yellow flags at patrolled beaches — surf conditions can change fast.

04

Outback driving means fuel stops can be 200km+ apart. Fill up whenever you can and carry extra water.

05

Public transport apps (like Opal in Sydney, Myki in Melbourne) make city-hopping simple and cashless.
