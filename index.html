Iconic Australian trips

Big-ticket routes to inspire your next escape — tap a trip for the stages.

3–12 months · ~15,000 km · Around Australia

  1. Stage 1

    East Coast run

    Sydney to Cairns — beaches, reef and warm-weather camps to ease into van life.

  2. Stage 2

    Top End

    Cape York detour, Kakadu, Litchfield and Darwin before the Kimberley.

  3. Stage 3

    The West

    Broome, Ningaloo, Coral Coast and Perth — the best sunsets on the lap.

  4. Stage 4

    Nullarbor & south

    Cross the Nullarbor, take in the Great Ocean Road and finish along the south coast.

10–14 days · 4WD · Far North Queensland

  1. Day 1–3

    Cairns to Cooktown

    Stock up, air down and tackle the coastal track past Cape Tribulation.

  2. Day 4–7

    The Old Telegraph Track

    Iconic creek crossings — Palm Creek, Gunshot and Nolans Brook.

  3. Day 8–10

    The Tip

    Stand at the northernmost point of the Australian mainland.

  4. Day 11–14

    Return via the OTT

    Fish the rivers, camp remote and retrace south at your own pace.

7–10 days · 4WD · The Kimberley, WA

  1. Day 1–2

    Derby start

    Air down and roll into the Kimberley's red-dirt heart.

  2. Day 3–5

    Gorge country

    Windjana, Bell, Manning and Galvans — swim beneath waterfalls.

  3. Day 6–8

    El Questro

    Zebedee Springs, Emma Gorge and station camping.

  4. Day 9–10

    Finish at Kununurra

    Lake Argyle sunsets and a well-earned cold one.

6–8 days · Remote 4WD · Outback SA/QLD/NT

  1. Day 1–2

    Prep at Birdsville

    Fuel, permits, sand flag and a Big Red warm-up run.

  2. Day 3–5

    1,100+ dunes

    Cross the parallel dunes convoy-style — the QAA and French lines.

  3. Day 6–8

    Dalhousie Springs

    Soak in the thermal springs and roll out to Mt Dare.

4–6 days · 4WD · Victorian Alps

  1. Day 1–2

    Mansfield to Craig's Hut

    Alpine tracks, mountain-cattlemen history and huge views.

  2. Day 3–4

    Wonnangatta & Blue Rag

    River-valley camps and the famous Blue Rag Range ridge run.

  3. Day 5–6

    Dargo & the Crooked River

    Historic gold country, creek crossings and quiet camps.

3–5 days · Beach 4WD · Queensland

  1. Day 1

    Barge across

    Air down for the world's largest sand island and hit 75 Mile Beach.

  2. Day 2–3

    Lakes & wrecks

    Lake McKenzie, Eli Creek and the Maheno shipwreck.

  3. Day 4–5

    Inland tracks

    Rainforest camps, Central Station and a final beach cruise.
